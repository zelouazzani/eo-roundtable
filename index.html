An exclusive Virtual Roundtable for EO Barcelona

Tax, Wealth & Your Next Move

Your wealth. Your family. Your tax strategy.
Thursday 19 November 202616:00 CETOnline · 90 min

Is the way you manage your wealth aligned with the life you want for yourself and your family? Join Christophe Suchy and Gail Warrior, entrepreneurs, YPO members and co-founders of Global Citizen Consulting, for a candid, 90-minute virtual roundtable exclusively for EO Barcelona members. As tax and residency rules change across Europe and beyond, we'll explore the trade-offs of staying or relocating, how you own your business and wealth, and what you pass on to the next generation. Shaped by your anonymous questions, this confidential, unrecorded conversation draws on real stories and peer experience, so you leave clearer about your options and the decisions that affect your wealth, your family and your future.

The session

An honest conversation among peers, in the spirit of Forum

No lecture and no pitch. No recording and no AI note-takers. Nothing leaves the room.

Who it is for

EO Barcelona members who live, work or invest across borders, or who are thinking about where they will live, how they own their business and what they will pass on.

The objective

Understand your options as tax and residency rules change across Europe, hear how your peers are handling the same decisions, and leave clearer on what matters for your wealth, your family and your future.

Our guests

Christophe Suchy and Gail Warrior

Co-founders of Global Citizen Consulting, members of YPO and CEO, and co-authors of The Global Freedom Blueprint. They made their own move in 2020, and they will open with that story.

Christophe Suchy
Co-founder, Global Citizen Consulting

Scientist, serial founder, advisor and board member. He has lived in Spain for 23 years and works between Europe and the United States, helping founders, executives and families design their international footprint: tax residency, second residencies, holding structures and family offices. He is also President of ReSPR Technologies, and a former professional volleyball player.

Gail Warrior
Co-founder, Global Citizen Consulting

A former Mobil Oil accountant, she founded Warrior Group in Texas and grew it from a $31K first year into a $124M modular construction company, then led CASPR Group until her exit in 2023. A certified executive life coach, she advises growth-stage founders on strategy and fundraising.
